The Archer Class

In the world of Mystic Tavern, the Archer is a master of precision and deadly accuracy. Armed with a trusty bow and an assortment of arrows, they rain down a flurry of projectiles from afar, swiftly dispatching enemies with their ranged physical attacks. With their keen eyes and nimble movements, Archers provide essential long-range support to their allies in the epic battles that unfold.

The Paladin Class

As the frontline defender in the realm of Mystic Tavern, the Paladin stands as a paragon of courage and strength. Clad in heavy armor and wielding a mighty sword or hammer, they excel in close-quarters combat, unleashing devastating melee physical attacks upon their foes. Paladins are renowned for their unwavering resolve, shielding their comrades from harm and leading the charge against evil forces.

The White Mage Class

Within the enchanting world of Mystic Tavern, the White Mage embodies the essence of healing and restoration. Armed with ancient magical arts and wielding a staff, they are masters of mending wounds and revitalizing their allies. White Mages channel the ethereal powers of light, mending broken bodies and replenishing vital energy, ensuring their team remains strong and resilient throughout their perilous journeys.

The Black Mage Class

In the mystical realm of Mystic Tavern, the Black Mage harnesses the raw power of arcane forces. Wielding a staff crackling with dark energy, they unleash devastating ranged magical attacks upon their adversaries. Black Mages weave intricate spells, conjuring destructive elements and manipulating the very fabric of reality to decimate their foes from a distance.

The Bard Class

Within the enchanting world reminiscent of Lord of the Rings, the Bard is a charismatic and versatile support class. Masters of both music and speech, they inspire their allies with uplifting melodies and captivating tales, bolstering their courage and enhancing their abilities. Bards excel at crowd control, manipulating the battlefield and providing crucial support, ensuring their comrades remain motivated and ready for any challenge that awaits in the mythical realm of Mystic Tavern.
